# AK-LÖWEN / Solingen — Design Concept V2

Static design reference only. No website implementation was created in this step.

## Basis
- Concept 03 **Mut in Bewegung** from the supplied 5-concept PDF.
- Colors and the existing generated model/photo world are intentionally unchanged in V2.
- The supplied orange/black AK-LÖWEN logo photo is used as the brand reference.

## V2 adds
- Updated information architecture and real training content.
- MMA & Sambo as one combined section.
- Dedicated VALSET acrobatics concept with Mama & Kind, Junior and Senior groups.
- Training schedule with confirmed times and visibly marked open points.
- Trial training/contact concept, Telegram/WhatsApp flow and DE/RU/UA/TR language scope.
- Impressum/Datenschutz structure with current-law notes; no final legal text is claimed.

## Open before implementation
- Prices / trial-training price.
- Exact gGmbH legal name, address, representative and register data.
- Boxing trainer name and verified bio.
- Final Saturday MMA/Sambo times (10:00–11:30 for children/youth is still treated as a conversation proposal, not as confirmed).
- Final real club photos and the final technical stack for Datenschutz/Cookies.
